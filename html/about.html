    Our Story

    Founded in 1998 in Mumbai, Xera Bank began with a simple mission: make quality banking accessible to every Indian household. What started as a single branch has grown into a nationwide network of 450+ branches serving over 2 million customers.

    Today, we combine the warmth of relationship banking with cutting-edge digital tools — so whether you visit us in person or bank from your phone, you always feel valued and secure.
