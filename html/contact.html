    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I open a savings account online?

    Visit our Savings Account page and click "Apply Now". You'll need your Aadhaar, PAN, and a valid mobile number. Account activation takes under 10 minutes.

    What are your home loan interest rates?

    Home loan rates start at 8.5% p.a. for salaried individuals. Women borrowers receive an additional 0.25% concession. View full details.

    Is my deposit insured?

    Yes. All deposits are insured by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank, as per RBI guidelines.
